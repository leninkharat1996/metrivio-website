ANALYTICAL THINKING

What a recommendation looks like.

ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE — NOT CLIENT DATA
Observation

Prospecting campaign has rising spend while conversion efficiency declines.

Evidence

CPA increased while click-through rate remained relatively stable.

Hypothesis

The issue may be occurring further down the funnel rather than at the ad level.

Action

Investigate landing-page CVR, offer alignment and audience quality before cutting the campaign.

Measurement

Compare conversion rate, contribution CAC and incremental performance after the intervention.
