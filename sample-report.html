ILLUSTRATIVE EXAMPLE — NOT CLIENT DATA
Example DTC Brand Monthly acquisition spend: $150k Diagnostic period: 90 days
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The account is generating growth — but allocation can be improved.

Overall efficiency 71/100
Potentially inefficient spend $18.4k
Opportunity identified $12.7k
Recommended reallocation $10k
Primary finding

Performance deterioration is concentrated in a small number of prospecting campaigns. The evidence suggests the issue is not simply media efficiency — funnel conversion and audience quality should also be investigated before making major budget cuts.

WHAT WE FOUND

Four decisions emerge from the analysis.

STOP

Campaign X

Spend increased while conversion efficiency deteriorated. Marginal performance is below the account benchmark.

Confidence: HIGH
FIX

Landing Page

Ad-to-page messaging is inconsistent with the strongest acquisition audiences, creating potential conversion leakage.

Confidence: MEDIUM
SCALE

Campaign Y

Strong marginal efficiency and stable conversion performance suggest additional budget can be investigated.

Confidence: HIGH
TEST

Creative angles

Three new creative hypotheses should be tested against the strongest existing audience and offer combinations.

Confidence: MEDIUM

FUNNEL & ECONOMICS

Media performance is only part of the diagnosis.

Funnel signals

CTR: broadly stable across the period.

Landing-page CVR: declining for the highest spend prospecting audience.

Checkout CVR: broadly stable.

AOV: stable, suggesting the primary issue is more likely acquisition and conversion efficiency than basket size.

Economic signals

CAC: increasing faster than contribution margin can comfortably absorb.

Payback: lengthening as prospecting efficiency declines.

Marginal efficiency: materially stronger in a smaller set of campaigns.

Implication: reallocating spend may create more value than simply increasing total acquisition investment.

30-DAY ACTION PLAN

What we'd do next.

01 Reallocate

Move a portion of marginal budget away from deteriorating prospecting campaigns and into stronger marginal opportunities.

02 Investigate

Validate landing-page conversion, audience quality and post-click behaviour before making permanent campaign decisions.

03 Test

Launch new creative and offer hypotheses against the strongest audience combinations.

04 Measure

Evaluate changes using contribution CAC, conversion efficiency and marginal performance rather than platform ROAS alone.
