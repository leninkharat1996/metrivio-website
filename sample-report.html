01 / Executive summary
The answer

You don't need to spend more. You need to make the existing $150k work harder.

The account is generating growth, but the next dollar is not equally valuable everywhere. The largest leak is concentrated in a small number of prospecting and automated acquisition pockets. At the same time, several campaigns are performing well enough to justify controlled expansion.

The opportunity is therefore not a blanket media cut. It is a reallocation problem — remove marginal spend where the evidence is deteriorating, repair the downstream conversion leak, and put the released budget behind stronger opportunities.

13 / Decision rules

A recommendation is stronger when we tell you what could prove it wrong.

These rules prevent optimisation from becoming opinion disguised as data.

STOP / REDUCE

Marginal efficiency stays weak

Reduce spend when CPA remains materially above the relevant benchmark after allowing for conversion lag, audience mix and attribution differences.

SCALE

The next dollar still works

Increase gradually only when incremental spend maintains acceptable marginal CPA and contribution economics.

FIX

The leak is downstream

Prioritise the funnel when traffic quality is stable but landing-page, checkout or offer conversion deteriorates.

TEST

The evidence is promising but incomplete

Don't scale an interesting signal. Create a controlled experiment with a clear success threshold first.

AMAZON

ACOS isn't enough

Evaluate paid marketplace sales against total sales, organic behaviour, SKU economics and inventory before increasing spend materially.

SHOPIFY

Revenue isn't enough either

Ultimately connect acquisition to contribution margin, new-customer CAC, repeat behaviour and payback where the data supports it.

14 / The Metrivio approach

Less reporting. More decisions.

Metrivio is built around a simple principle: a marketing report is only valuable if it changes what the business does next.

01 / DIAGNOSE

Find the leak

Identify where spend, conversion or customer economics are deteriorating.

02 / CHALLENGE

Question the obvious answer

Separate media problems from creative, funnel, measurement and economic problems.

03 / QUANTIFY

Put money behind the finding

Translate the evidence into an opportunity size and an allocation decision.

04 / PRIORITISE

Choose what happens first

Rank actions by impact, confidence, effort and reversibility.

05 / TEST

Prove the hypothesis

Define success and failure before making the change.

06 / REALLOCATE

Put the next dollar to work

Use the evidence from one cycle to make the next allocation decision.
