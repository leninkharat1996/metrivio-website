02 / What we found

Four decisions emerge from the analysis.

The diagnostic is designed to produce decisions rather than another collection of charts.

STOP Confidence: HIGH

Campaign X

Spend increased while conversion efficiency deteriorated. Marginal performance is below the account benchmark.

Reduce marginal exposure and redirect the budget into controlled tests.
FIX Confidence: MEDIUM

Landing Page

Ad-to-page messaging is inconsistent with the strongest acquisition audiences, creating potential conversion leakage.

Align message, offer and landing experience before scaling traffic.
SCALE Confidence: HIGH

Campaign Y

Strong marginal efficiency and stable conversion performance suggest additional budget can be investigated.

Increase budget gradually while monitoring marginal performance.
TEST Confidence: MEDIUM

Creative angles

Three new creative hypotheses should be tested against the strongest existing audience and offer combinations.

Run controlled creative tests before making broad account changes.

04 / Funnel & economics

Media performance is only part of the diagnosis.

A channel can look inefficient because of the media itself — or because something downstream is weakening the economics. The diagnostic separates those possibilities before recommending a major budget move.

Implication

Reallocating spend may create more value than simply increasing total acquisition investment. The strongest opportunity is to improve the quality of the marginal dollar rather than chase more volume.

06 / 30-day action plan

What we'd do next.

The objective is not to make every possible change. It is to make a small number of high-confidence changes, then measure what happens.

01

Reallocate

Move a portion of marginal budget away from deteriorating prospecting campaigns and into stronger marginal opportunities.

Improve marginal efficiency
02

Investigate

Validate landing-page conversion, audience quality and post-click behaviour before making permanent campaign decisions.

Confirm root cause
03

Test

Launch new creative and offer hypotheses against the strongest audience combinations.

Create new winners
04

Measure

Evaluate changes using contribution CAC, conversion efficiency and marginal performance rather than platform ROAS alone.

Validate impact
07 / The Metrivio approach

Better marketing decisions start with better diagnosis.

Metrivio connects the decisions that are often analysed separately: media allocation, funnel conversion and customer economics.

01 / MEDIA

Assess marginal performance

Identify where additional spend appears to create value and where performance is deteriorating.

02 / FUNNEL

Find downstream leakage

Separate media problems from landing-page, audience and conversion issues that may be distorting acquisition performance.

03 / ECONOMICS

Connect spend to business value

Evaluate CAC, contribution economics, payback and marginal efficiency before recommending a major allocation change.

Important limitation

This page is an illustrative example, not a claim about actual client performance. Actual recommendations depend on the quality and availability of the underlying business and marketing data. Where evidence is incomplete, the diagnostic should identify the gap rather than present an assumption as fact.
