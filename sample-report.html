02 / Decision board

If this were the account, these are the four decisions we'd make first.

The purpose of the diagnostic is not to produce more reporting. It is to reduce the number of decisions the growth team has to make.

STOP HIGH CONFIDENCE

Reduce deteriorating prospecting

Spend is increasing while conversion efficiency is deteriorating. The marginal dollar is becoming less productive.

ACTION → Reduce marginal exposure first, not the entire campaign.
FIX MEDIUM CONFIDENCE

Investigate landing-page leakage

CTR remains relatively stable while landing-page conversion weakens. The issue may sit after the click.

ACTION → Validate audience, offer and landing-page alignment.
SCALE HIGH CONFIDENCE

Increase stronger marginal opportunities

Selected campaigns are delivering stronger marginal efficiency and should receive controlled incremental budget.

ACTION → Increase gradually and monitor marginal CPA.
TEST MEDIUM CONFIDENCE

Test new creative and offers

New creative hypotheses should be tested against the strongest audience and offer combinations rather than launched broadly.

ACTION → Run controlled tests with predefined success criteria.

07 / Funnel diagnosis

The problem may not be where the dashboard says it is.

A declining platform metric does not automatically mean the media needs to be cut. We follow the customer through the funnel before deciding where the intervention belongs.

01 / Acquisition Paid traffic Meta · Google
02 / Engagement Landing page CTR · CVR
03 / Commerce Shopify AOV · CVR
04 / Marketplace Amazon Sales · ACOS
05 / Economics Customer value CAC · LTV · Margin

What the evidence currently suggests

CONFIRMED SIGNAL

CTR remains broadly stable while landing-page conversion has weakened for the highest-spend prospecting audience.

HYPOTHESIS

Audience quality, offer alignment or landing-page experience may be responsible for part of the deterioration.

BUSINESS IMPLICATION

Cutting the entire campaign could remove profitable volume before the actual cause of the problem is established.

NEXT TEST

Compare landing-page CVR, contribution CAC and marginal performance before and after the intervention.

10 / Measurement plan

How we'd know the recommendations actually worked.

Recommendations are not considered successful because a dashboard turns green. They are evaluated against the business outcome they were intended to change.

01 / MEDIA

Marginal CPA

Did the additional dollar produce customers at an acceptable marginal acquisition cost?

02 / FUNNEL

Landing-page CVR

Did conversion improve after correcting the suspected post-click issue?

03 / ECONOMICS

Contribution CAC

Did the intervention improve acquisition economics rather than simply platform-reported ROAS?

11 / The Metrivio approach

From marketing data to decisions.

Metrivio's process is deliberately structured around diagnosis, challenge, quantification, prioritization and measurement.

01 / DIAGNOSE

Analyse the full picture

Paid media, funnel, Shopify, Amazon and customer economics.

02 / CHALLENGE

Test alternative explanations

Separate media problems from creative, funnel and measurement problems.

03 / QUANTIFY

Connect to economics

CAC, AOV, margin, LTV and payback where the data allows.

04 / PRIORITIZE

Rank the opportunities

Focus attention on impact, confidence and effort.

05 / MEASURE

Define the test

Every recommendation gets a measurable outcome.

06 / DECIDE

Make the next allocation

Turn evidence into the next budget and growth decision.

Important limitation

This page is an illustrative example, not a claim about actual client performance. Actual recommendations depend on the quality, completeness and consistency of the underlying marketing, ecommerce and business data. Where evidence is incomplete, the diagnostic should identify the gap rather than present an assumption as fact.
