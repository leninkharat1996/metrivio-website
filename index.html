THE PROBLEM

Your dashboards show performance. They don't show decisions.

Meta, Google, Amazon and Shopify each report on themselves. None of them tell you that a campaign with a strong ROAS is quietly eroding margin, that your top-of-funnel spend is masking a checkout problem, or that the "winning" channel only wins because of how it's measured.

Most founders don't have a spend problem. They have a visibility problem — no independent view that connects media, funnel and customer economics into one decision.

Metrivio exists to close that gap — with one independent read across every channel you're actually spending on.
