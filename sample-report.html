02 / What we found

Four decisions emerge from the analysis.

The diagnostic is designed to produce decisions rather than another collection of charts.

STOP Confidence: HIGH

Campaign X

Spend increased while conversion efficiency deteriorated. Marginal performance is below the account benchmark.

Reduce marginal exposure and redirect budget into controlled tests.
FIX Confidence: MEDIUM

Landing page

Ad-to-page messaging is inconsistent with the strongest acquisition audiences, creating potential conversion leakage.

Align message, offer and landing experience before scaling traffic.
SCALE Confidence: HIGH

Campaign Y

Strong marginal efficiency and stable conversion performance suggest additional budget can be investigated.

Increase budget gradually while monitoring marginal performance.
TEST Confidence: MEDIUM

Creative angles

Three new creative hypotheses should be tested against the strongest audience and offer combinations.

Run controlled creative tests before broad account changes.

08 / 30-day action plan

What we'd do next.

The objective is not to make every possible change. It is to make a small number of high-confidence changes, then measure what happens.

01

Reallocate

Move a portion of marginal budget away from deteriorating prospecting campaigns and into stronger marginal opportunities.

Improve marginal efficiency
02

Investigate

Validate landing-page conversion, audience quality and post-click behaviour before making permanent campaign decisions.

Confirm root cause
03

Reconcile

Compare platform-reported performance against Shopify revenue, Amazon sales and customer economics to identify measurement gaps.

Improve decision confidence
04

Test

Launch new creative, offer and audience hypotheses against the strongest combinations.

Create new winners
05

Measure

Evaluate changes using contribution CAC, conversion efficiency and marginal performance rather than platform ROAS alone.

Validate impact
09 / The Metrivio approach

Better marketing decisions start with better diagnosis.

Metrivio connects the decisions that are often analysed separately: media allocation, funnel conversion, ecommerce performance and customer economics.

01 / DIAGNOSE

Analyse the full picture

Analyse paid media, Shopify, Amazon, funnel behaviour and customer economics.

02 / CHALLENGE

Test alternative explanations

Separate media problems from creative, measurement, funnel and economic problems.

03 / PRIORITIZE

Turn evidence into action

Rank opportunities by likely impact, confidence and effort, then define how each should be tested.

Important limitation

This page is an illustrative example, not a claim about actual client performance. Actual recommendations depend on the quality, completeness and consistency of the underlying marketing, ecommerce and business data. Where evidence is incomplete, the diagnostic should identify the gap rather than present an assumption as fact.
