11 / What happens next

The first 30 days are about proving the decisions.

Every recommendation needs an intervention, a measurement criterion and a decision rule. Otherwise it isn't a strategy — it's an opinion.

01

Days 1–3 — Remove obvious inefficiency

Reduce Meta Prospecting C07 and Google PMax Generic according to the recommended allocation. Preserve high-intent demand capture while avoiding a wholesale account reset.

Immediate efficiency
02

Days 4–10 — Repair conversion leakage

Audit the highest-spend landing pages, message-to-page alignment, mobile experience and offer friction. Build two controlled variants.

Improve CVR
03

Days 7–14 — Scale proven pockets

Increase Meta Retargeting C03 and Google Brand Search gradually while monitoring marginal CPA rather than blended account averages.

Capture efficient demand
04

Test creative angles, landing-page combinations and Amazon Sponsored Products against explicit hypotheses.

Create new winners
05

Compare contribution CAC, CVR, marginal CPA, Amazon incrementality and Shopify economics. Reallocate the next tranche only where the evidence supports it.

12 / Decision rules

What would make us change our recommendation?

This is where the analysis becomes genuinely decision-ready. A recommendation should include the evidence that could prove it wrong.

STOP / REDUCE

Weakening efficiency

Reduce if CPA remains materially above benchmark after accounting for conversion lag and audience mix, with no compensating economic signal.

SCALE

Proven marginal efficiency

Increase only when incremental spend maintains acceptable marginal CPA and does not materially deteriorate contribution economics.

FIX

Downstream leakage

Prioritize funnel intervention when traffic quality is stable but landing, checkout or offer conversion deteriorates.

TEST

Interesting but unproven

Do not scale hypotheses simply because early performance looks promising. Create a controlled test with a clear success threshold.

AMAZON

Incrementality matters

A lower ACOS alone is insufficient. Additional investment should be evaluated against total marketplace demand and organic sales behaviour.

SHOPIFY

Economics matter

Paid-media decisions should ultimately be evaluated against customer acquisition cost, contribution margin and payback rather than platform ROAS alone.

13 / The Metrivio approach

From marketing data to decisions.

Metrivio is designed around the same principle as the homepage: diagnose the problem, challenge the obvious explanation, quantify the opportunity, prioritize the next move and measure what happens.

01 / DIAGNOSE

Find the waste

Identify spend, campaigns, audiences, creatives and funnel stages that deserve attention.

02 / CHALLENGE

Find the why

Separate media problems from creative, funnel, measurement and economic problems.

03 / QUANTIFY

Size the opportunity

Connect performance to CAC, AOV, margin, LTV and payback where data allows.

04 / PRIORITIZE

Choose the next move

Rank opportunities by likely impact, confidence and effort.

05 / MEASURE

Prove or disprove it

Define what success looks like before making the recommendation.

06 / REPEAT

Reallocate again

Use the resulting evidence to decide where the next marketing dollar goes.
