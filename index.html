BEFORE YOU BOOK

The practical questions, answered.

What exactly is the technology/system fee?

It is the fixed fee for the infrastructure required to operate the acquisition system, including prospecting, research, data, outreach infrastructure, automation, tracking, qualification, scheduling and reporting.

What is the performance fee?

It is a separate fee tied to qualified commercial sales calls that meet the agreed qualification criteria and actually happen.

What counts as a qualified call?

The company fits the agreed target profile, the conversation involves an appropriate decision-maker or buying influencer, there is a legitimate commercial HVAC opportunity, the meeting is scheduled and the prospect attends.

Do I pay if the prospect does not show?

The performance fee is tied to attended calls. A scheduled no-show is not an attended sales call.

Do you guarantee closed deals or revenue?

No. Metrivio is responsible for targeting, prospecting, qualification and appointment setting. Whether a meeting becomes a project depends on your sales process and the buyer.

Who finds the prospects and decision-makers?

Metrivio handles target-account research, contact identification and the outbound process based on the ICP and service geography we agree together.

Do I have to provide the list?

No. Your input helps define the market and exclusions, but the prospecting and research system is operated by Metrivio.

What happens after someone books?

The qualified meeting lands on your calendar. Your team takes over the sales conversation, while the agreed tracking and reporting keeps the process visible.

What types of commercial HVAC work can you target?

Potential targets can include replacement, rooftop work, retrofit, maintenance contracts, service agreements, new construction, facility expansion and aging-equipment opportunities.

Can this work in my service area?

That depends on market density, your service geography, your commercial offering and the type of accounts you can realistically serve. We assess fit before recommending a rollout.

How much involvement is required from my sales team?

The main requirement is being able to take qualified calls and handle the commercial sales process. We can align on the handoff and information needed before launch.
